# Using your telescope helper

1. Turn on the telescope box. Wait one minute.
2. Open **Astro** on the tablet.
3. **Hold the big circle** and talk. Let go when you finish.
4. Try: **"What's good tonight?"** — then **"Go to Saturn"** or **"Next."**
5. Push the telescope the way the voice and the big arrow say.
   When you hear **"stop"**, let go. A dot means you are on it.
6. Say **"Stop"** any time.

The screen is red to protect your night vision. Tap **Day mode** to change it.
