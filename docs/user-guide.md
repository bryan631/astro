# Using your telescope helper

**Start**

1. Turn on the telescope box. Wait one minute.
2. Open **Astro** on the tablet.
3. **Hold the big circle** and talk. Let go when you finish.
   The first time, it asks to use the tablet's location. Tap **Allow**.

**Find something**

4. Say **"What's good tonight?"**, then **"Go to Saturn"** or **"Next."**
5. Push the telescope the way the voice and the big arrow say.
   When you hear **"stop"**, let go. A dot means you are on it.

**Take a picture**

6. Say **"Take a picture."** If it asks, turn the focus knob slowly
   until it says **"that's the sharpest"**, then say **"Done."**
7. Keep your hands off the telescope until it says the picture is ready.
8. Tap **Pictures** to see them.

**Good to know**

- Say **"Stop"** any time.
- Put in or took out the Barlow? Say **"Barlow in"** or **"Barlow out."**
- Moved the telescope to a new place? Say **"Use my location."**
- The screen is red to protect your night vision. Tap **Day mode** to change it.
